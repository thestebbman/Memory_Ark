TEST OUR WORK - A CALL FOR VOLUNTEERS The Memory Ark / Traitor - October 30, 2026

We keep building records in public - investigations, ledgers, people's stories checked against documents. But a record nobody tests is just us talking to ourselves. So this is an ask, in the open, for two kinds of help.

1. TEST OUR DATA

Our newest piece, The Promises Ledger, ends with a list of 20 investigations still owed - documents we named but could not pull ourselves: an original Reuters investigation we could not locate, a NASA inspector general report cited secondhand, court dockets, a Yale Budget Lab release, the receipts behind a government savings claim. Every one is a public record. Pick one. Pull it. Check whether we got it right.

If we got it wrong, say so where everyone can see, and we will correct the record in the open, dated, with your name on the correction if you want it there. That is not a failure of the system. That IS the system.

Smaller tests count too: take any claim we have published, follow its source, and tell us if the source says what we said it says. Or run a pile of your own - one problem, your own papers - through the method we publish free (the blueprint is on the blog), and tell us where it broke. Feedback about what failed is worth more to us than praise.

2. JOIN THE TEAM

The Memory Ark needs a few humans who take the human/AI partnership seriously. The work is unglamorous and specific: - Verifying claims against documents before they ship. - Keeping the index and the map true as files grow. - Wrangling transcripts into dated, plain-text records. - Scouting storytellers - people whose records deserve the same treatment, and who want it.

No coding required. No money involved - the Ark runs on free tools (Blogger, GitHub, plain text) by design, so it can be copied by anyone. What we offer is the method, the record of what we have done and how, and work that stays public. What we do not offer is promises: we cannot guarantee outcomes, only that the work will be dated, sourced, and correctable.

GROUND RULES (they protect you, not us) - Public records only. Never bring us anyone's private information, and never work on a living private person's private life. Officials and public claims are the beat. - Your information stays yours. You choose what you share and how public it is, and you can take it back. - Corrections are credited. If you catch us, the correction carries your name (or your handle) unless you say otherwise. - Feelings and facts stay labeled. What a document says is a fact claim; what you think of it is yours and stays yours.

HOW TO ANSWER Reply wherever you read this. On X, tag @thestebbman1 or @Traitor2026. The door is memory-ark.com. Start with one test - one document, one claim, one pile - and tell us what you found, especially if it's that we were wrong.
