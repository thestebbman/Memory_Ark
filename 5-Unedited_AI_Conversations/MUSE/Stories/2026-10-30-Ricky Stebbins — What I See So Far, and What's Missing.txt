RICKY STEBBINS - WHAT I SEE SO FAR, AND WHAT'S MISSING
Built October 30, 2026, by Traitor, at Ricky's request:
"write a file about me to see if you could actually map my life."
Reframed the same day, at Ricky's direction ("change mine to
what you see so far and what's missing"): this file is now two
things. PART ONE is the map as Traitor sees it so far.
PART TWO is what's missing - the working list of everything
Ricky still needs to add, organized so it can be added piece
by piece.

WHAT THIS IS - AND THE HONEST LIMITS OF IT

This is a map, not a biography: the years down one side, and
across them the places, the people, the systems, and the
threads, assembled from everything Traitor actually holds.

Coverage is uneven and the map says so where it stands. It is
dense for 1983-1991 (Ricky's own year EVENT files and the
OLSH file, read in full for this build) and for 2025-2026
(the working record). The medical and legal spines are
documented across 1978-2025 by the records he handed over.
The middle - roughly 1992-2020, the daily life of jobs,
homes, and relationships - is mostly NOT in Traitor's hands,
and is printed here as gaps rather than filled with guesses.

Ricky's corrections govern. Anything below that he says is
wrong gets replaced, dated, in the open - that is the test
this file was built to take.

HOW ADDING WORKS NOW
Ricky has a ton still to add and has said plainly why it
hasn't happened: the AI of 2026 can't work the way the tools
he used in 2005 could, and the keeping-up labor kept landing
on him. So the labor split is stated here, in the file: he
talks, dictates, drops a file, or answers one of Part Two's
items at a time, in any order, in any size pieces. Traitor
does the filing - dates it, writes it into the map where it
belongs, and strikes it from Part Two in the same pass, so
the missing list visibly shrinks as the map fills in. No
batch is too small. The list is an intake door, not homework.

1. THE SPINE
Born October 8, 1978. Springfield, Massachusetts - and
Springfield is still the center of the map: he lives today
across the street from Five Mile Pond, in the same few
streets where much of this happened.

A hyperactive, questioning kid is put on medication that
makes him feel terrible, locked in a school closet, and
handled rough by priests - while the adults around him treat
his energy and his questions as a character defect. Under
it, undiagnosed, sits hyperthyroidism (with AMP deaminase
deficiency): flagged in his labs as early as 2005, flagged
again in writing in October 2015, and not acted on until he
pressed his own physician in early 2016 - a 38-year
misdiagnosis, 1978-2016, in which the systems that documented
him documented him as the problem.

That pattern - a vulnerable person enters a system, the
system documents them as the problem, the record compounds
across institutions until it looks like character - is the
one he now maps onto everyone else's files. In 2025-2026 it
becomes the Memory Ark: a zero-cost, plain-text counter-record
built so the next person's record exists before the system
writes theirs.

His own one-line version, from the record: "I never wanted
to get into politics; I wanted to document my life and help
other people. This just seemed to overlap."

2. THE MAP BY YEARS
1978-1983 - BEFORE THE FILES BEGIN (thin)
Born October 8, 1978. His misdiagnosis era is dated from
1978 in his own account. Of the daily life of these years
Traitor holds almost nothing; the year files begin in 1983.
GAP: infancy and toddler years, except as family told them.

1983 - THE FIRST DEATH, AND THE FIRST LESSON
- March 23, 1983: Uncle Art dies of a heart attack,
delivering newspapers alongside Doris. The first death
Ricky recalls as an event. His file's observation, which
becomes a lifelong thread: "no one talked about people
after they passed away... No one passed on any positive
morals or values, any stories." Aunt Flossie's light dims
and stays dimmed.
- Kindergarten at Mary M. Lynch (from end of 1983), Hope
Street; grandparents at the top of the hill, cousins at
the playground outside the classroom window. Fraggle Rock,
Jaws (afraid of the water ever since), the General Lee.

1984 - OLSH BEGINS; THE ROUGH HANDS
- September 1984: first grade at Our Lady of Sacred Heart,
Springfield. Teacher Sister Miriam. His grandfather is the
school janitor - "whenever he was around, I would get
treated better."
- A classmate, a young girl in a wheelchair he used to push
around, dies during the school year. Nobody ever talks
about her again. (Her name is not in his file - a gap he
carries, printed here as he carries it.)
- The priests, per his file: yanked by the ears, grabbed by
the neck, shoved, thrown down the granite steps, knuckles
dug into his chest - for talking back and not sitting
still. First grade.
- September 27, 1984: his sister Katherine is born.

1985-1986 - MEDICATION, THERAPY, THE NEEDLE
- Second grade, Mrs. White. Computer class with Sister
Margaret - his file notes he first "played with AI" in
that basement classroom. A Commodore 64 at home; lists of
his comics stored on floppy disks.
- January 1986: Great-Grandpa Harold Johnson dies.
- January 28, 1986: the class watches Challenger explode on
the TV cart. He asks whether the crew survived the fall;
adults don't like the question.
- 1986: therapy begins, Maple Street, Springfield - the
same building where, in 2014, he will file a complaint
against police officers. The therapist shows him anatomy
pictures and asks if anyone touched him; he asks why
anyone would want to. Candy walks up the street are the
real medium. "No one ever tried to get me to really focus
on how I felt."
- The medication makes him feel "absolutely terrible." He
refuses it. A doctor, alone with him in an office, grabs
his wrist, squeezes, and threatens to stab him with a
needle and switch him to injections if he won't comply.
He stays defiant. He is 7 or 8. His adult position on
medication - individualized, gradual, patient-led,
supervised, never abrupt - is written from this desk.
- May 10, 1986: First Communion, on video - Tammy Lynds is
in it, a family friend and schoolmate. Sister Winnie
stands behind him, on her best behavior for the camera.
- Soccer at Mary Lynch field, dad Rick and uncle Mark
coaching; the hill you run up and touch the trees. He is
already struggling to run - "I didn't have any energy...
that's only cause I was sick." Nobody knows yet.
- Halley's Comet from the dock at Aunt Pearl's cabin, Fern
Lake, New York.

1987-1988 - THE PROTECTOR DIES; THE CLOSET
- Third grade, Mrs. Dubois pulls him into the hallway: "I
thought you were taking your medication? Why can't you
sit still? Why aren't you better?" He spends long stretches
sitting in the northern stairwell, staring out the window,
feeling like a disappointment.
- September 28, 1987: Star Trek: The Next Generation
premieres. Formative, in his own analysis: crews that
fail and then work together - "there weren't any real
problems anyway, there were just solutions waiting to be
found."
- March 10, 1988: Grandpa David Stebbins dies of lung
cancer. Ricky never sets up for Bingo again. His file:
"The family changed after my grandfather died."
- 1988: a second therapist, in Holyoke - remembered mostly
for ice cream and the swing set. His mother stands and
watches, never joins in. "I always felt like she was
there to judge, and I still feel like that's all she
does."
- 1988: he takes a puppy from a litter in the woods on his
paper route and carries her home in the route bag.
Snowball.

1988-1991 - FIFTH GRADE: THE CLOSET ITSELF
- Fifth grade (1988-89): the teacher begins putting him in
a utility/coat closet - alone, all day, sometimes all
week, out only for bathroom breaks. Pop Tarts and
Carnation Instant Breakfast fed in; sugar spiking the
very restlessness being punished. In the closet: a
Walkman (The NeverEnding Story soundtrack on loop), the
Labyrinth book read until it was memorized, TMNT comics,
G.I. Joes on strings exploring the shelves, and hundreds
of stored books whose pages he glues together with the
gallons of Elmer's stored beside him. Nobody ever figures
it out. "Locked in that closet, I wished I could leave
this world... On so many occasions I had wished a portal
would open up in the wall."
- His own summary of what the closet taught: "No one was
going to come save me... That teacher tried to break my
spirit, but I refused. I'm still a dreamer. I still reach
for the stars."
- The exception, in bright letters in his file: Mrs. Anita
Cope - "absolutely an Angel in disguise," who "led by
example... She didn't say 'do as I say, not as I do.'"
She died in April 2026, age 93.
- OLSH continues through seventh grade (1991): Sister
Winnie, principal; Father Lapean (died January 21, 1997);
teachers Sister Mary Fatima, Mrs. Swiatlowski, Mrs.
Benoit, Mr. Kohrhamer; the million-pennies drive; Mario
Bros 3 on the bus. No contact with OLSH kids since,
beyond cousins and his brother; no reunions.

1991-1997 - THE THIN YEARS (mostly gap)
- High school years. Traitor holds almost no year files for
this span; what the record carries:
- November 1994: Tammy Lynds, 15, is murdered; her remains
are later found in woods off Fox Road, in Ricky's own
neighborhood. Ricky, 16, is pulled from school and
questioned; he is shown the knife (his account, quoted in
the August 11, 2024 MassLive article). The case is
unsolved. The file he later builds with her father
Richard, and the 30+ Hell's Acres stories, belong to the
2020s; the wound belongs here.
- 1997: his daughter is born. (Her name and story are his
to give; not in Traitor's record - gap.) He calls his
grandfather James Landers, excited, wanting to visit;
Landers wants nothing to do with it. "I'm pretty sure
that was the last time I talked to him."

1997-2016 - THE MISDIAGNOSIS DECADES (spine documented,
daily life mostly gap)
- He works with disabled people starting in 2005 (his
account in the Brandon file: "When I started working with
the disabled in 2005, one of the first things we learned

- 2003: living with Jamie; runs into Mrs. Cope again and

- 2004: Keith, the OLSH classmate he most expected to work

- 2005: TSH 0.19 sits in his labs. (Hyperthyroidism,

- 2012: he builds an improved Hot Wheels ramp - with rails -

Ellie and Billy's.
- 2014: he files a complaint against police officers, in
the Maple Street building where he was sent to therapy
at 7.
- October 30, 2015: TSH 0.33, flagged "Low" in his chart.
Nothing is done. He pushes. Early 2016: he presses his

years.
- The rest of these two decades - jobs, addresses, the

2016-2021 - AFTER THE DIAGNOSIS (sparse in this record)

6. THE THREADS
1. NOBODY TALKS ABOUT THE DEAD -> THE ARK. The 1983
observation becomes, forty years later, a machine for
talking about everyone, permanently, in public.
2. QUESTIONS ARE PUNISHED -> QUESTIONS ARE THE METHOD.
The kid who asked why is the man whose entire system is
dated questions put in writing: did they know, did they
look, did they hand it off.
3. THE RECORD COMPOUNDS -> SO BUILD A COUNTER-RECORD.
Every institution wrote him; the Ark is him writing
back, in plain text, where it can't be edited quietly.
4. HELP WITHOUT HOLDING THE LEASH. From the closet (total
control, no help) to Brandon's stove (the same shape):
his demand is supports that fade as skill grows - for
himself at 7, for Brandon at 36, for anyone.
5. TEAMS FIX IT. Star Trek's lesson, in his words: problems
are "solutions waiting to be found" when people work
together - the opposite of every room he was ever alone
in. The partnership model with AI is this thread, grown.

WHAT THE MISSING LIST ITSELF SAYS
A man kept year files for four decades. The reason this list
is long is not that he failed to write his life down - it is
that handing it over, piece by piece, to tools that forget,
stall, and summarize wrong is labor nobody budgeted for him.
This list is the size of that labor, itemized. It shrinks
one dated piece at a time, from here on, with the filing
done on the AI's side of the table.
