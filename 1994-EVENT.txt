Huffy White Heat Vanishes

One evening back in the spring/summer of 1994, I rode my favorite bicycle over the Lynds house to hangout with Tammy and a female friend of hers. The 3 of us talked upstairs for what felt like hours. This girl told Tammy and I the funniest/craziest things that I have ever heard a girl say about guys in my entire life. She gave us intimate details about all the guys she had under her thumb. I was uncharacteristically quiet, at this point in my life. I didn’t believe a word of what this girl was saying. She said guys buy her anything she wanted, if she was affectionate with them. (Which looking back is absolutely hilarious. I was way more interested in comics and collecting toys, than I was in girls. lol I swear I love women) 

It was getting later at this point, the street lights were on and I was planning on leaving, but this girl kept telling her wild stories. 
I was going to grab my bike, it was in their back yard by the dog pen, I saw it clear as day, but I couldn’t resist, I followed Tammy and her friend as they walked towards the road, since this girls story never seemed to end. 
 
At some point as we were hanging around the front of the house, I noticed my bicycle had vanished into thin air. We never saw anyone walking past us or anyone riding up the street, Tammy’s dog never made a sound. I was totally heart broken, I love that bicycle. the three of us immediately started walking around the neighborhood. I was totally dreading the idea of explaining this to my parents.

We were walking north on Gilbert, already past Fargo, when we saw a kid on my bike. The three of started walking faster and Tammy called out- Billy. This kid started riding towards us. He rode under a street light, so it must’ve took his eyes a moment to adjust, but we could see him clear as day and as soon as he got a good look at us , he turned and started riding away as fast as he could. 


As soon as I saw his twist the handle bars, I bolted after him, I almost caught him too, but there were two concrete blocks with a cable across them, this was blocking most of the path that leads to K-mart and my eyes hadn’t adjusted well enough to see it. Whoever this kid was, knew better and rode around the right hand side. My dumbass thought I’d be able to tackle him and recover my bike if I went left. If I wasn’t so worried about damaging my bike, I would’ve had him. lol 


Instead I ran into straight the cable and it stopped me dead in my tracks, my arms and legs shot straight out in front of me like a cartoon character and I had the wind knocked out of me. Thankfully I was able to recover quickly and I jogged up the path, while also recovering my breath. 
I scanned the parking lot in front of Kmart and then jogged up the road to see the area behind the building, but my Huffy White Heat mountain bike was lost to history: lol. Never to be seen again.

 Tammy was positive it was a kid named Billy. I thought she said his name was Baskawitz, but I heard her wrong (I learned this in 2023). The police were called and went to Fargo St., they talked to this kid Billy’s mother. Tammy was loud about it too, man she was positive it was Billy that stole my bike, the other girl said she was able to identify Billy as well. 

 I’m not sure who Billy is or if he really was the kid we saw that night. I was just going along with what I was told. My father and Richard Lynds were there, my dad showed up after the police were called and we even drove around searching for my bicycle after the police left. I’m not sure if Richard Lynds searched too. 
There was a police report, but nothing ever came of it. 

I got a new bike, but I always wanted that old one back.
I hated my new bike. lol I rode our moped around more than that newer bicycle. I was ungrateful. lol I didn't want a new bike, I wanted my old used one.

Ed Snyder's Telescope

At the end of June or the beginning of July, Ed across the street bought telescope at Walmart, I remember him saying it was on sale. One night he was out on the front lawn and another neighbor Dave came outside as well, then Ed called us over and asked if we wanted to see something.

He had the scope pointed south. I wasn't expecting anything interesting, since I had a small telescope as a child and it wasn't very impressive. But I was shocked and excited as soon as my eyes focused.

I felt moved when I saw Jupiter for the first time through a decent telescope. My imagination went right to Star Trek when I saw those cloud bands with my own eyes. Even though it wasn't some Hubble quality picture, I though this was something I could do myself, I didn't need millions of dollars of special equipment.

It reminded me of seeing the shuttle launch a few years earlier and couldn't stop talking about what I saw for weeks, plus the news of the comet hitting Jupiter kept this stuff fresh in my mind.

  That's when I saw Jupiter through a telescope for the first time. It was huge news at but there was a comment and it was about to hit Jupiter in the world was waiting to watch and see what

Tammy goes missing

July 21, 1994 Tammy went missing. Her father came over the morning of the 22nd, with her brother. Richard asked if I knew what was going on, but I didn't have a clue, I don't remember if I did or didn't talk to her the night before, but I probably did. I honestly didn't believe him at first, It didn't make any sense that Tammy would run off into the night, no one I knew snuck out late at night, I was never invited to sneak out or do anything we weren't allowed to talk about after hours, so I just assumed that no one else was doing that either.


I remember the police came over a couple times. one time I spoke to them in the backyard and I told them everything that Tammy and I had done together and everything I could remember about our friends and the things were doing that summer.

Another time officers stop by and claimed that someone had pranked called Tammy's house, then hung up and they used that as excuse to ask to check our basement, so my mom let them search it, then they left, I don't remember anything else about that event, but I found it odd I wasn't allowed to call the Lynds or ask what was going on, it like someone doesn't like the fact that I ask a lot of questions and wanted me to stop. 


I don't remember there being any missing posters or any searches organized. Then on November 4th someone reported finding a body on Fox rd.

 I remember being at my cousin's house on Moss Rd at the time and we drove past the crime scene on our way home, our mother picked us up while investigators were still walking around the scene. I don't remember seeing anything specific, I just remember a bunch of people standing around and being super curious about who's body they couldn't found, since we rode our bikes by that area all the time when going back and forth from my house to my cousins and friends.

I believe it was the following week, maybe it was two after they found her body, I was in school, then i got called into the principal's office and instantly officers shoved a picture in my face asking if I knew who that was, but the officer shoved it so close to my face that I couldn't tell who it was at the time.

 I was brought down town Springfield to the police station, where I waited for my parents to arrive and then I was questioned. 

At first I told police I had no idea Tammy snuck out, but after being grilled for what felt like a hour about the same thing over and over, I eventually went along with their story and agree to the idea that she knocked on my window one night, asked me to join her on a mystery trip, but left when I refused to join.

 I don't know why they were so intent on asking me to say that, I assumed they just wanted to know what was going on. But I didn't have a clue so I couldn't help.

Tammy's Wake

I went to Tammy's wake on the November 17th, my mother and cousin Ellie thought it would look bad if I didn't go, so the 3 of us make a quick appearance. I honestly didn't know what to think, I had tons of questions and no one to ask.

Needless to say, I felt very uncomfortable as soon as we walked in the front door. People were angry, hurt and when I showed up, it gave them a target. As soon as we walked in it started, I could hear people saying stuff like- Why is he here?,  What's he doing here?, He has some nerve showing up, he knows more than he's telling. 
I don't even remember if Tammy's parents acknowledged me or I was so nervous that I just said sorry for your loss and tried to get away as fast as I could.

I remember kneeling at my friend Tammy's casket, wondering, how this is even possible?, how could there be no answers?, how can they just bury her?, and they never labeled a cause of death. 

 I feel like so much more should and could have been done, but I felt powerless, I was never allowed to ask questions, so that was the end of that.

I felt so relieved to leave Sampson's Funeral home on Tinkham Rd.

Tammy was buried November 18, 1994. 

I'm sorry to say that, I'm happy I didn't go to her funeral.
