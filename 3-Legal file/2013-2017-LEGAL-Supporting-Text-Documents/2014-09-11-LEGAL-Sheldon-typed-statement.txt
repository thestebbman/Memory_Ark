towards Boston rd. From the angle I couldn't see the license plate. I got another look at the driver and raced to get dressed but by the time I got outside the black ford was to far away to see the license plate but it took a left onto Wisteria St. There was a lot of snow on the ground and the street was very icy. My neighbor Richard stebbins was crossing boston rd and wasn't to be found when I drove up the street. 

The next day I called the Springfield police and did a wellness check on my neighbor because I hadn't seen him since the fight in the street.
