SECTION 8: WHAT THIS FAMILY TREE MEANS FOR THE CASE

The original 2022 and 2024 DHHS intakes name Tamzi as the parent of concern. What they do not show — because DHHS never investigated far enough to find it — is the family surrounding Tamzi.

Zack and Kenzo are living in proximity to:
— Their maternal grandfather (Lester), who pled guilty in 2012 to reckless endangerment in the death of a child their grandfather was responsible for
— Their maternal grandmother (Amaryah), who attempted to have that child's death certificate changed
— The extended family network that coached children to lie during the 2009 investigation and which has, by Eddie's account, repeated that pattern in his own case

The DHHS Safety Assessment of October 2024 was closed because an eight-year-old autistic child said he was safe. The assessment did not identify who was in that child's household. It did not run a background check on any adult in that household. It did not connect the case to the 2009 Spokane County record. The same worker who closed the 2022 intake in one day closed this one.

That is the pattern. That is what the Ark documents.
