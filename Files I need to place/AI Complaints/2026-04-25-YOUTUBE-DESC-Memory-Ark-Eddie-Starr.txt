WHO IS LUMINARY STARR

Eddie Starr — known in his community as Luminary Starr — is a father of two boys: Zack (born 2016, autistic, active IEP) and Kenzo (born 2021). He served in the United States Navy. He has been fighting for his sons for five years.

His case involves:
— A family history that begins with a four-year-old child's death in Spokane, Washington in 2009
— A 2012 guilty plea for reckless endangerment in that child's death — public record, Spokane County case 09-1-04325-7
— Two DHHS neglect investigations in Nebraska — one closed in a single day despite documenting his children living on a junk yard with chainsaws, barbed wire, and no running water
— A court order that barred his evidence from trial because of a discovery procedural failure
— DHS draining his bank account when he confronted them about fraud instead of investigating
— A grandfather who is a substitute teacher and mandated reporter who has failed to file required reports at least two, possibly three times

He also admin a Facebook group of 13,000 members spread across the United States, United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand, and South Africa.

He was fighting alone. Now he is not.

THE TITLE IV-D CONNECTION

Eddie figured this out on his own, years before this meeting. He named it clearly in this conversation: states profit from placing parents on child support enforcement. The federal money flows under Title IV-D, originally designed for cases of actual abandonment. What it became is something else. It became a revenue mechanism. That mechanism is why lawyers, courts, and state agencies have a financial interest in keeping parents separated from their children.

Ricky's project documents this pattern across jurisdictions. Eddie is not an isolated case. His case is one entry in a national ledger.

THE GRANDFATHER

Eddie mentioned this quietly near the end of the meeting. The maternal grandfather in this case is a substitute teacher. That makes him a mandated reporter under state and federal law. A mandated reporter is legally required to report known or suspected child abuse regardless of family relationship, regardless of whether they believe it will be handled, regardless of anything.

Eddie knows of at least two occasions, possibly three, when the grandfather was told about abuse and did not file the required report. That is not a family disagreement. That is a documented failure of a legal obligation.

This is in the record now.

THE MEMORY ARK NETWORK

The Memory Ark Network is a documentation project. It is not a law firm. It does not charge money. It does not give legal advice. It does not pretend to be something it isn't.

What it does: it indexes the stories of people who have been failed by institutions — courts, CPS, DHHS, schools, law enforcement — and makes those stories permanently public and machine-readable, so that the pattern cannot be dismissed as individual error.

Eddie's story now belongs to the Ark.
