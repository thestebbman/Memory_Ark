Use Transcription: During a meeting, you can start a live transcript. After the meeting, a copy of the transcript is automatically saved to the host's Google Drive.
2. Closed Captions (CC):

Live captions are available in Google Meet and can be turned on during any meeting to display the conversation as text on the screen in real-time.

Turn on Captions: During your meeting, click the "Turn on captions" button at the bottom of the screen.
