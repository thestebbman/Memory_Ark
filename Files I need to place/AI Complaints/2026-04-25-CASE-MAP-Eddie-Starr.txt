JUNE 2023 — Dodge County, Nebraska
Tamzi LaFleur's attorney Richard Register serves discovery requests on Eddie Starr.
Case: CI23-20, Tamzi LaFleur v. Eddie Starr.
Eddie does not respond.
Source: Court order, October 2, 2023.

OCTOBER 2, 2023 — Dodge County District Court, Nebraska
Judge issues Order on Plaintiff's Motion to Compel.
Finding: "The Defendant has willfully failed to answer the discovery of the Plaintiff."
Order: Answer discovery by October 6, 2023, or evidence will be barred.
Result: Evidence barred. The photos of the junk yard, the DHHS report, documentation of the hazardous conditions — barred from introduction at trial.
Source: Court Order, Case CI23-20, District Court of Dodge County, Nebraska.

WHAT THIS MEANS: The court barred Eddie's evidence from this proceeding because he did not follow discovery procedure. This is a procedural loss, not a factual one. The evidence still exists. It is not inadmissible in future proceedings, in appeals, in complaints to oversight bodies, or in public documentation. The barring order only governs what can be presented in that specific case.

Division of Children and Family Services

Lincoln, NE 68509
