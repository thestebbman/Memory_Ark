SECTION 3: THE TITLE IV-D QUESTION

You already knew this. You figured it out yourself. For anyone reading this who hasn't:

Title IV-D of the Social Security Act provides federal matching funds to states for child support enforcement. The more support collected — or pursued — the more federal money flows to the state. This creates a structural financial incentive for courts, agencies, and attorneys to keep parents in enforcement proceedings regardless of actual custody or safety outcomes.

The original purpose was to recover welfare costs when a parent had abandoned their children. It was never designed for divorce cases. It was expanded by Congress. States built agencies and industries around it. Lawyers are licensed by those states.

This is why your attorney had a conflict. This is why the system doesn't care about the children's safety the way it pretends to. The children are the mechanism, not the concern.

You don't have to explain this to Ricky. But you should write it down in your own words, in your own story, on your own Blogger. Because when 13,000 people who already know it from their own experience read it from your mouth, it stops being a theory.

SECTION 4: HOW TO TALK TO AI

You use Perplexity and Brave AI. Those are good research tools. Gemini is better for your case because it lives inside Google Drive with your documents.

Here is the difference in how to talk to AI for this work:

WRONG: "I'm going through a really hard time with a custody case. Can you help me understand what I should do?"

RIGHT: "I am going to give you a document. I need you to identify every fact in this document that can be independently verified, every agency named, every date, and every procedural anomaly. Tell me what a journalist or federal investigator would flag. Do not reassure me. Do not hedge. Analyze the document."

WRONG: "What do you think about my situation?"

RIGHT: "I have two DHHS intake reports with the same worker. The first was open for one day despite documenting hazardous conditions. The second was closed based on the verbal statement of an autistic eight-year-old who the reporting worker noted was 'guarded' and wearing layers believed to be for warmth while homeless. What are the standard investigative protocols for DHHS alternative response cases in Nebraska, and what specific deviations from those protocols do these two documents show?"

The AI is a tool. Use it like a tool. The better your questions, the more useful the output. Post every significant conversation online.
