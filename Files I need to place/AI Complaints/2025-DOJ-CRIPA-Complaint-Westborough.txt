SUMMARY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

From approximately July 19 to July 30, 2025, I was involuntarily committed to Westborough Behavioral Healthcare Hospital for twelve days. During that time, I was subjected to conditions and staff conduct that violated my constitutional rights and the rights of other patients on Unit B.

This complaint is supported by the facility's own internal Human Rights Complaint resolution documents, which confirm the facts stated herein. These are not allegations the hospital disputes — they are documented findings the hospital itself adjudicated.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
INCIDENT 1 — POLICE ACCESS BLOCKED, INJURY DOCUMENTATION PREVENTED
Internal Human Rights Log #19 | Complaint Date: 7/23/2025 | Resolution: "Presents undisputed facts."
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Prior to my admission at Westborough, I was physically assaulted by medical personnel at Baystate Medical Center when I attempted to use my phone to record staff for my own protection during a forced transfer. I arrived at Westborough with visible injuries from that restraint.

At Westborough, I attempted to:
(a) photograph my injuries for documentation, and
(b) contact Springfield Police to request they come to the facility to document my injuries.

Staff at Westborough prevented both actions.

The facility's own Human Rights Officer investigated this complaint and classified the resolution as "Presents undisputed facts." The resolution document confirms that staff had not been following proper procedure and that the Human Rights Officer was required to educate staff that patients have the right to contact police at any time. Staff were also educated that patients may use their phones to photograph injuries.

The significance of this finding cannot be overstated: an involuntarily committed patient who arrived at the facility with injuries from a prior assault was prevented by staff from contacting law enforcement and from documenting those injuries. The facility confirmed this happened. This constitutes a deprivation of my Fourth and Fourteenth Amendment rights and a violation of Massachusetts patients' rights law (M.G.L. c. 123, §23).

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
INCIDENT 2 — MEDICAL NEGLECT: KNOWN THYROID CONDITION IGNORED
Internal Human Rights Log #14 | Complaint Date: 7/21/2025
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

I have a documented thyroid condition that requires medication at a specific dosage. The dosage administered by Westborough staff was incorrect, and when I reported this, staff failed to address it with the prescribing provider. This resulted in days of improper medication for a condition directly connected to my mental health stability and the reason I was committed.

The Human Rights Officer confirmed the complaint by informing nursing staff of the issue. The delay in addressing a known, documented medical condition in an involuntarily committed patient constitutes deliberate indifference to a serious medical need, in violation of clearly established constitutional standards under Estelle v. Gamble, 429 U.S. 97 (1976).

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
INCIDENT 3 — STAFF INCITING PATIENT-ON-PATIENT VIOLENCE
Internal Human Rights Log #25 | Complaint by: Jonathan Croteau | Complaint Date: 7/28/2025
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

A fellow patient, Jonathan Croteau, filed a Human Rights complaint on 7/28/2025 alleging that staff on Unit B were actively encouraging other patients to fight him on 7/26/2025. The Human Rights Officer forwarded this to nursing leadership for investigation.

I witnessed staff behavior consistent with this complaint during my admission. The deliberate incitement of violence between involuntarily committed psychiatric patients by institutional staff represents a serious constitutional violation and a failure of the facility's duty of care.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
INCIDENT 4 — STAFF PHYSICALLY ASSAULTING A PATIENT DURING RESTRAINT
Internal Human Rights Log #27 | Complaint by: Elson Dos Santos | Complaint Date: 7/29/2025
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Patient Elson Dos Santos filed a Human Rights complaint on 7/29/2025 alleging that he witnessed staff beat another patient's head against the floor during a restraint on 7/27/2025. The complaint was forwarded to the Director of Risk Management.

I was present on Unit B during this period. The use of excessive force during a restraint — specifically slamming a patient's head against the floor — constitutes a violation of the Eighth and Fourteenth Amendments and potentially constitutes criminal assault. The facility's response of forwarding to Risk Management rather than contacting law enforcement suggests a pattern of internal suppression of serious misconduct.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PATTERN AND CONTEXT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

These incidents did not occur in isolation. My admission to Westborough was itself the product of a civil rights violation: I went to Worcester court to file a harassment order against Commonwealth Care Alliance (CCA) for their documented failure to provide mental health services. Instead of processing my filing, the court ordered a psychological evaluation and had me transported to Baystate Medical Center, where I was physically assaulted before being transferred to Westborough. The involuntary commitment was used as a direct response to my attempt to use the civil court system.

This pattern — court system redirecting a pro se litigant into psychiatric detention rather than hearing their complaint — is documented in my broader archive at:
https://github.com/thestebbman/Memory_Ark

The Westborough incidents are part of a documented, decade-long pattern of Massachusetts state institutions responding to my attempts to seek accountability with suppression, detention, or dismissal rather than remediation.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

I am requesting that the DOJ Civil Rights Division, Special Litigation Section open a CRIPA investigation into Westborough Behavioral Healthcare Hospital regarding:

1. The systematic blocking of patient access to law enforcement and injury documentation.
2. The use of excessive force during restraints.
3. Staff incitement of patient-on-patient violence.
4. Deliberate indifference to known serious medical conditions.
5. The use of involuntary psychiatric commitment as a response to civil court filings.

The facility's own internal documents confirm items 1 through 4. I am prepared to provide full documentation and to cooperate fully with any investigation.
