Dear Mr. President,



I desperately need your help. I have been speaking out for years about corruption in the Massachusetts court system and the abuse of disabled individuals, but I keep getting ignored.



In 2013, I was on workers’ comp when I was hit by a car in a Walmart parking lot in Springfield, MA. The driver had no insurance, a rejection sticker for safety, and a suspended registration, facts the police knew but deleted from my report. Instead of taking my statement, they arrested me, denied me medical attention, and let the driver leave. After my release, I went to the emergency room, wrote a statement, and had it notarized before my court appearance.



I thought this was a misunderstanding, but the system worked against me. I was falsely convicted of malicious damage to a motor vehicle after Judge William Hadley lied about my jury being dismissed. ADA Katherine Johnston altered my notarized statement, entered a fake receipt as evidence, and violated Brady disclosure laws. Judge Hadley helped cover it up. At the time, I was sick and weak from an undiagnosed genetic muscle disorder and hyperthyroidism. In 2015, I was charged with forgery to silence my investigations because I wouldn’t stop fighting for the truth. I attempted suicide three times, and in 2016, after having a gun placed to my forehead outside my lawyer’s office, I was coerced into pleading guilty to the forgery charge, despite my innocence and having physical evidence to prove it.



Since 2017, I have also fought to expose the abuse of disabled individuals in Massachusetts. I worked in group homes before all of this started and witnessed firsthand the severe neglect and abuse within these state-funded programs. I filed multiple reports with the Disabled Persons Protection Commission (DPPC) over the years, but they were ignored. One example: Tammy Hyland, a state Human Services Coordinator, helped cover up abuse, while her husband, Mike Hyland, runs Venture Community Services, which profits from these same disabled individuals.



In 2021, I was falsely accused of assault. For the past three years, the Worcester County DA’s office and my court-appointed lawyers have conspired to block my trial because they know I intend to expose the corruption behind my case. They have deliberately withheld bodycam/dashcam footage from my arrest and pretended it doesn’t exist. I have been documenting everything and posting court pretrial audio on YouTube under Ricky Stebbins (profile image: the moon).



I have reported these crimes to the Massachusetts State Police, Springfield Police, Springfield Mayor Sarno, Governor Healey’s office (which refused to help), and the Attorney General (who claimed they don’t investigate crimes by state employees). The Supreme Judicial Court, run by Healey’s ex-lover, has also ignored my complaints, further ensuring that no one is held accountable.



I have been on disability since 2018, but I refuse to sit back and do nothing. I could give up, but I won’t. The Massachusetts court system is corrupt, and our services for disabled adults are disgraceful. I know I can make a difference if someone listens. These people are standing in my way.



I have been dragged through the dirt for decades. Most of my family and friends think I’m stupid and wasting my time. People have yelled at me, telling me to quit and go on vacation. I have suffered needlessly because of all of this, but I have kept going because I know what I’m doing is right and believe with all my heart that I am going to make a difference. By addressing these issues, you won’t just be helping me, you will be helping every citizen in Massachusetts and giving a voice to disabled people who cannot speak for themselves.



Please help me make a difference.



Sincerely,

Ricky Stebbins
