🏦 THE ACQUISITION


In 2010, Cerberus Capital Management—a private equity firm named after the mythological three-headed dog guarding the gates of hell—purchased Caritas Christi Health Care from the Archdiocese of Boston for approximately $895 million. The deal required approval from then-Attorney General Martha Coakley, who imposed conditions: maintain charity care, preserve services, reinvest in facilities. These conditions had no enforcement teeth. No clawbacks. No penalties. Just trust.

💰 THE EXTRACTION MODEL

1. Debt Loading: The $895M purchase was financed largely with borrowed money. That debt was placed on Steward’s books—meaning the hospitals themselves were responsible for repaying the cost of their own acquisition.
