In 2013, I was struck by a car while walking through a parking lot. Despite being injured, responding officers denied me medical attention. I went to trial without legal representation and was found guilty. Afterward, I discovered that the case had been mishandled at multiple levels. The officers involved were Thomas Sheehan and Thomas Douglas. The prosecutor was Assistant District Attorney Katherine Johnston with the help of Edward Kavari. The presiding judge was Bildo Hadley, and the court’s head clerk was Jon Gay. Several attorneys either mishandled my case or refused to represent me, including Randy Milo, Eric Applebaum, Anna Levine, John Nolan, and Elaine Pourinski. I submitted complaints and outreach to numerous oversight bodies, including the Massachusetts State Police, the FBI, the Massachusetts Attorney General’s Office, the U.S. Department of Justice, the Governor’s Office, and the Board of Bar Overseers. None of these institutions took corrective action. This case illustrates systemic failure across local law enforcement, prosecution, the judiciary, legal defense, and state and federal oversight agencies.
