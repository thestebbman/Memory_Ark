2. Explain How They Work

    RICO: 1) Identify enterprise/pattern (2+ acts in 10 years); 2) File in federal court (4-year SOL); 3) Prove injury to business/property; 4) Remedies: treble damages/injunctions [Jenner Guide, 2021; USSC Primer, 2020].
    FCA: 1) Whistleblower files sealed qui tam suit; 2) DOJ investigates/intervenes; 3) If successful, recover triple damages/penalties; 4) Reward claim within 90 days of Notice [Zuckerman Law, 2024; DOJ Manual, 2023].
    MA FCA: 1) File with AG or court; 2) Prove knowing submission of false claims; 3) AG may intervene; 4) Recover triple damages/penalties up to $11K/claim; 5) 2025: Disclose ownership for PE liability [MA AG Site, 2025; Arnold Porter, 2025-02-06].
    FCC Complaints: 1) Submit online/via mail with station info, distortion details; 2) FCC reviews/investigates; 3) Possible fines/revocation if violation found [FCC.gov, 2024; Brookings, 2025-02-25].
    SEC Whistleblower: 1) Submit TCR form anonymously; 2) SEC investigates; 3) If sanctions >$1M, apply WB-APP within 90 days; 4) Reward 10-30% [SEC.gov, 2024; Phillips Cohen, 2024].
    FOIA: 1) Submit to agency (e.g., HHS portal); 2) Describe records; 3) Agency responds in 20 days; 4) Appeal denials [FOIA.gov, 2024; CMS Instructions, 2024-09-10].
