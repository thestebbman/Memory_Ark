1995-1-25

 

Summer

It was some we're around this time that someone had gotten by a train on the railroad tracks over by Five Mile Pond and for whatever reason Mindy and I went for a walk over there, and it was pretty gross. You could still see chunks of flesh everywhere and I saw what looked like an elbow or shoulder joint along the tracks.


August
 Right around this time I bought a 1987 Mercury Grand Marquis. It looked like an old person car, it had tons of space and I loved it. It had a 5.0 engine, not that I really understand what that means even now, lol but it was fast and handled very well. 



I also got a job as a prep cook at Olive on the corner of Boston Road and Parker Street, This is the first job where ever had any real responsibility, But it sucked working alone.

 I thought it was a lot of fun making the different sauces. I learned how to make Alfredo, it was simple prepping the meat sauces, cooking the chicken tenders and meatballs. Though I did tap the inside of the machine we cooked the meatballs in once, and oh man did that hurt like crazy. lol
I got to have all the free soup, salad and breadsticks I wanted as well. Though I mainly ate breadsticks and banana pepper. lol

September

During this soccer season, We had a game next to cemetery and I remember my cousin Ken found some monster bouquet of flowers and brought it over to his mom in the middle of game and she was mortified. lol 
My Aunt Sandy Hollered at him to return it, he probably found it in a pile, I doubt he stole it off of somebody's grave. lol It was probably from compost piles, I know cemeteries collect plants that people leave on their visits.
