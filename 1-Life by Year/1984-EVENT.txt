

1984
work-in-progress

This was still a very carefree time for me. I don't recall feeling stressed or overwhelmed by anything. I do remember coloring and playing with craft paper. 

Though this was the first time a classmate or a child had died around me, just like Uncle Art's death, nobody wanted to talk about it. 

There was a young girl in a wheelchair who I interacted with on a regular basis. I got to push her around, and I remember feeling all proud of myself after being thanked for being so helpful. I recall liking the fact that someone needed my help, or maybe it was because I got recognition or that I got chosen for the responsibility of helping, but I remember it feeling like it was an honor to help; then she passed away sometime towards the end of the school year. I don't remember what issues she had or anything about her; she was just gone, and nobody ever talked about her again.




This is also he first time that I really remember getting in trouble for anything.

 I can still picture the scene today. lol. I was standing in line to the water fountain. There was a little boy in front of me bent over getting a drink, and for whatever reason I decided to knee him in the rear end,  then he bumped his head inside the water fountain. lol 

He started crying, and I got in trouble. lol 
I have no idea why I did this. I'm not sure what else was going on at the time; maybe I saw this in a movie and thought it was funny. lol I just remember doing it; I can't give you the motivation behind it at the moment; it's been 40 years. Needless to say, whatever punishment I received didn't deter me from any wacky behavior in the future.




I took the picture above of my old classroom in July 2026. At some point, they replaced the old water fountain; it used to be set into the wall. I'm shocked that I don't recall those windows running along the ceiling. (top left) I usually remember weird features like this, but I know I was always obsessed with the playground; that would be to the right of this picture and across the small parking lot. The new playground isn't as nice in my opinion. I miss the monkey bars and jungle gym. 
They also had the same type of curved tables when I went there, though it's hard to see in this pic.


I don't remember graduating from Mary M Lynch, or having any type of send-off to first grade, like we did in preschool. I don't recall ever seeing any of these kids again. 




Summer


===
🏫 Our Lady of Sacred Heart (OLSH) – Springfield, MA




September

I was excited to start going to school at O.L.S.H., Our Lady of Sacred Heart Catholic school.
I knew my grandfather worked there as a custodian, and I knew my cousins Michael and Steven already went there as well.  The way the adults talked about going here, I couldn't wait to start first grade, plus my brother and cousin Joey were also starting kindergarten this year; a family friend, Tammy Lynds, also went there. It seemed like such a special event; I thought I was going to get closer to God. lol Needless to say, this place didn't help me get as close to God as I had imagined. I learned some interesting life lessons in my time here.

It's not all bad, but I did get to experience the principal's office and priests roughing me up on countless occasions.

O.L.S.H. was a huge change of pace for me, and I wasn't prepared for how strict these teachers were compared to the teachers at Lynch. 

My sister Katherine was born September 27th

This was an exciting time. I remember driving with my dad and Bobby to the hospital and getting to hold Kathy for the first time. 

I remember the drive to my Aunt Flossie's right before my mom came home. My dad dropped us off in Ludlow on Ray St. We got to hang out with our grandma Francis while they got things ready. It's funny being excited for something that doesn't do anything. Great, we have a sister, but now what? lol

There was a family party when my mom got home, and I remember Bobby running around, then he got in trouble for stepping on Kathy in the living room. lol I don't know if he really stepped on her or my mom was just overreacting, but it was still funny. lol 

I also got to introduce Kathy to my class; it was celebrated as an event, so this time felt like it was special. Though I did notice the excitement ended fast, and it was back to boring right away. lol

===
October

I got sent to the office a lot in first grade. For talking back too much. Because I couldn't sit still. The priest would yank me by my ears; he would shove me around for talking back; he'd pull me by my arms when he thought I wasn't listening; he'd grab my neck and yank me around like I was a ragdoll. I was thrown down those granite steps in the front of the building more than a couple of times. 
The priest would push me up against the wall and dig his fist into my ribs and his knuckles into the center of my chest, telling me that I needed to sit still and stop talking. 

My first-grade teacher, Sister Miriam, had her work cut out for her. This is the first year I recall melting crayons on the steam radiators. I also got in trouble for drawing all over my papers all the time. I even got sent to the office for pulling the chair out from under a classmate. I think it was Karen. I can't recall where I saw that, but I thought people would think it was funny. It's not so funny when you're on the receiving end. 


It was around this time that my dad used to force me to eat peas, and I hated them. I would almost throw up. I don't react like that at all now. I think it was because they were canned and had a funny taste. 


It was around this time I remember we got an Atari and got hooked on playing video games


I have some vague memories of our family picnics and trips to New York to Fern Lake and maybe even Batavia to see my grandfather. I'll have to get back to these early ones. 

December

I remember getting Optimus Prime that year at Toys R Us, and my brother got Shockwave. We got to go to the store ahead of time and let our parents know what we wanted. Like all the other kids, I fell in love with Transformers at first sight. I thought the cartoon was awesome, and once I got my own toy, I felt like I was going to save the world. lol
