

1984

This is the first time did a classmate or a child had died around me and nobody wanted to talk about it. There was a young girl in a wheelchair who I interacting with on a regular basis, I got to push her around and I remember being thanked for being so helpful, but she passed away some time during the school year, I don't remember what issues she had or anything about her, she was just gone and nobody ever talked about her ever again.

This is the first time that I really remember getting in trouble for anything, I was standing in line at the water fountain and there was a little boy in front of me getting a drink and for whatever reason I decided to knee him in the rear end and then he bumped his head inside the water fountain and he started crying and I got in trouble. lol I have no idea why I did this, I'm not sure what else was going on at the time, if I saw this in a movie and thought it was funny. lol I just remember doing it, I can't give you the motivation behind it at the moment, it's been 40 years. lol


I don't remember graduating from Mary M Lynch, or having any type send off to first grade, like we did in preschool. 



Summer


🏫 Our Lady of Sacred Heart (OLSH) – Springfield, MA




September

I was excited to start going to school at OLSH Our Lady of Sacred Heart Catholic school.
I knew my grandfather worked there as a custodian, and I knew my cousins already went there as well Michael and Steven. I was starting first grade and my brother and cousin Joey were starting kindergarten, a family friend Tammy Lynds also went there. It seemed like such a special event, I thought I was going to get closer to God. lol 



My sister Katherine was born the 27th
This was an exciting time, I remember driving with my dad and bobby to the hospital and getting to hold Kathy for the first time. My dad dropped us at my aunt Flossie's after this and we got to hangout with the grandma Francis while they got things ready.
there was a family party when my mom got home and I remember bobby running around and then getting in trouble on Kathy in the living-room, I don't know if he really stepped on her or my mom was just over-reacting, but it was still funny. lol 

I also got to introduce Kathy to my class, it was celebrated as an event. 

October

I got sent to the office a lot in first grade, for talking back too much, because I couldn't sit still. The priest would yank me by my ears, shoved me, pull me by my arms, they'd grab my neck and yank me around and I was thrown down those granite steps in the front a couple of times. The priest would push me up against the wall with their fists digging their knuckles into my chest telling me that I needed to sit still and stop talking. first grade teacher sister Miriam
Well I remember my dad used to force me to eat peas and I hated them and I would almost throw up. Like that at all now I think it was because they were canned and had a funny taste. It was around this time I remember we got an Atari started playing video games and we had our family picnics and trips to New York to Fern Lake and maybe even Batavia to see my grandfather

December

I remember getting Optimus Prime that year at Toys R Us and my brother got shockwave, we got to go to the store ahead of time and let our parents know what we wanted and I fell in love with Transformers at first site, the cartoon was awesome and once I got my own toy, I felt like I was going to save the world. lol
