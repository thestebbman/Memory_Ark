
 1988
I think we were a little young at the time, but I loved that our cousin Ellie used to let us watch all kinds of R-rated movies like Alien, Aliens, Predator, Rambo, and The Terminator ; 



I don't know how old kids should be before being allowed to watch violent movies. I thought it was great, and I don't think it affected me in a negative way, but I could be wrong. I don't recall ever emulating stuff I saw in those movies.

Around this time, the family was watching Mike Tyson fights on pay-per-view and wrestling events.

At the end of this year, Nintendo Power came out. I was beyond excited when I got a subscription from my parents; I think it was for my birthday. I also got the Dragon Warrior game and became obsessed with beating it. I used to love reading the magazine and following along. I didn't care about figuring out how to beat it on my own; I was lazy and just followed the directions. My cousins on Moss Rd also had a subscription, and we talked about them all the time.





March 10, 1988

Grandpa Stebbins passed away. I don't remember feeling sad. I was young and dumb. I thought that since he was in heaven, all was fine. 

I was such a dork as a kid. lol. At his wake, I remember asking Jeremy and Steven why they were wearing school uniforms, and they replied that he would have liked it, and I remember wondering if he really would have. I myself was wearing a red and black flannel while sporting a mullet. lol 

I don't even remember being present enough to realize he was sick. I don't recall being able to understand what it all meant. I had forgotten he had surgery, and things like lung cancer didn't mean anything to me. 

I'm not sure how old I was when I first started to truly grasp concepts like death, and I'm also not sure when I started to be present for situations. There have been many times when I was physically there but not mentally, and times I'll never forget because I was truly present and aware in the moment.



Looking back, I wonder if I annoyed my dad when his father passed away. I didn't truly grasp the situation and thought that praying to God would make everything okay. I was big into the Transformers still, and seeing that Optimus had come back to life, I got it in my head that it might be possible to pray to bring back someone you love. You just have to have faith, and I did. lol I prayed so many times to God to bring my grandpa back. Since that kind of stuff is written about in the Bible, I really felt like I had a chance. But I was too young or dumb to really understand anything that was really going on; I actually thought praying would bring people back. lol 



I never stayed to set up for Bingo again after he passed away.

I need to look for more family picnic or New York videos or pictures







July Bronx Zoo

My grandma Stebbins and Aunt Mary took my cousin Jeremy, Andrea, and me to the Bronx Zoo. They took my brother, Joey, and someone else to the Statue of Liberty. I can't recall where Michael and Steven got to go. 

I never thought about how hard it must have been for her to take all of us grandkids on these trips. Especially so close to her husband's passing. I remember the bus ride there felt so exciting; we got to see a couple of cars on fire. It was like we were driving into a movie scene. 

I took a bunch of pictures while we were there and lost most of them over the years. I can recall annoying my grandma and having to be spoken to on more than a few occasions, but I can't remember what I was doing to irk her. lol I had a lot of energy back then, so I could have just been acting weird.

I did take a picture of someone's baby while we were standing in line. lol I remember thinking he was cute, though I have since lost that picture. I always wondered who he grew up to be. 

Some of the groundhog tubes were full of pee, so we couldn't climb in the best ones. I don't know what little kids were doing or why there was so much pee, but I was super disappointed we didn't get the good views. 

I don't remember being super excited to do anything here. I didn't appreciate the trip. But in my defense, I was just told that I'm going, and there was no point to it. I was never close with my grandma Stebbins or my Aunt Mary. i don't recall being excited about this trip or looking forward to another. This was also the only trip with Grandma Stebbins or Aunt Mary.

The bus ride back was uneventful. 








Snowball was so tiny when I took her. Looking back and can describe what I did as stealing a puppy, but in my defense they were all with the mother in the woods. Other kids were also grabbing puppies, and no one ever came looking or had to return any of them. Though some kids' parents refused to let them keep their puppy. I was delivering newspapers at the time and had my bag on me. I put her in the bag and finished my route. She was so small that she was unsteady when she walked. I'm pretty sure she was too young to be taken from her mother, but off she went anyway. lol 

She was so much fun to play with. I used to put her in a motorcycle helmet and push her around the house. I had to clean the kitchen floor after the helmet left a green streak across the entire kitchen, but it was worth it. lol

October

Holyoke therapist 
The things I remember most about this place were going to the cafeteria to get ice cream and playing on the jungle gym and swings they had outside. I was always able to get some kind of snack, and my favorite was those strawberry-flavored ice cream things with the crunchies on a stick. I'll have to look up what the hell they're called, man; I still eat those too. lol I also got to have those single-serving ice cream cups. Looking back, these people really loved to sugar me up. 


I loved playing outside on their play set. I thought it was so cool; it was in the middle of their field. I didn't care that it was just the two of us or that the therapist never ran around. 
I don't remember having any interest in chatting about anything they wanted to talk about; I just remember I had tons of energy, and these people never explained what they wanted, so I just talked about school and what was going on at home. I remember seeing my mom just standing around watching; she never got involved, she never offered her 2 cents, she never tried to help. I always felt like she was there to judge, and I still feel like that's all she does. lol

November

I don't remember when they started using the closet as punishment, but I felt like once my grandfather had passed away, I didn't have much protection from the adult nonsense. None of the adults cared what happened to any of the kids. If you were accused of doing something, everyone assumed you were guilty. lol Plus, I did do things to get in trouble too, so I didn't help things.


There were exceptions; I remember an older colored woman. lol I feel weird saying that; she was black.  Mrs. Cope was absolutely an Angel in disguise (I ran into her again in 2003 while living with Jamie, and I introduced her to my son and dog Hailey.)
Anyway, she was always very kind and very patient with me. She was nothing like the other teachers or nuns.

Looking back, I feel like Mrs. Cope was one of those people who didn't tell you how to behave. She led by example; she showed you how to be a better person. She didn't say "do as I say, not as I do", like the other crabby-ass adults. lol 
(I am fixing this 2023-10-14. I looked up Anita Cope; she passed away in April of 2026. She lived to be 93 and got to see her great-great-grandchildren. That's awesome. I can just imagine how many amazing stories she could have shared. It makes me happy to read that "she carried her signature joking wit until the moment Jesus called her home". She was such a kind woman.)


December
This is the year that I learned that Santa wasn't real, and up until this point I remember I truly believed in him. I can't remember who told me, but I remember not quite arguing, but seriously debating Santa's existence with another school kid and totally not believing him when he said to check my parents' closet. At some point after that, my brother and I started sneaking into our parents' closet; I showed him how to open and reseal gifts. I didn't feel like there were any problems with taking a little peek ahead of time, then closing them back up. lol Sure, I led the way, but my brother was a follower. 

I also remember that they held a talent show in the hall under the gym. I don't remember participating at all. I remember being terrified of the idea of standing up in front of my classmates. A couple of kids pretended to be in a band, and one kid, Matt, played the guitar, but it didn't have strings. 
I remember being impressed and thinking it looked super cool. lol

The Closet

I didn't spend the entire year in the closet. lol 
I also remember sitting in the back, in like a coat closet area, so that I wouldn't disturb the class. One time I recall we were taking a test on all the presidents and their terms of office. I remember needing to cheat because I couldn't remember all these years and names. I had so much anxiety that I couldn't sit still, let alone remember that nonsense. So I took advantage of the fact that I was out of sight and wrote all the answers down and got 100 on the test. I remember people being proud of me and saying good job, but also saying things like, " Why can't you do this all the time?" lol I think it's hilarious that I can picture myself slipping that paper out to cheat like I'm standing over my shoulder. I was such a sneak.




I can't remember exactly when the closet started, but it sucked. 
 I felt like such a loser sneaking out of the closet during recess time just to watch the kids playing outside from the window in the basement.

Locked in that closet, I wished I could leave this world.
I used to sneak all kinds of stuff in there with me. I was never bored; I felt like my imagination was on fire. 

I brought GJ Joe figures in there and used string as a method for them to travel and explore different shelves. I didn't have jet packs for them all, so I needed to get creative. lol

Starcom was another toy brand I brought into the closet to explore the depths of my imagination. These were awesome cause they were small and had magnets on them, so I could attach them to the shelves while on missions. 

 
I have always loved reading comics, and when this new series, Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles Adventures comics, came out, I became obsessed; I carried them everywhere. I thought the artwork was beautiful and imagined going on adventures with them. Though I don't recall anyone else being as interested in that series as I was.

I also used to bring my Walkman into the closet and listen to music all the time. I made mix tapes with all of my favorite songs. I used to sit around for hours waiting for my favorite songs to come on so I could record them. There were a lot of commercials on some tapes. lol

I was bored out of my mind while in that closet. I picked through everything. One time I got the bright idea to glue all the pages of their books together. lol
They stored all the extra books and supplies in that closet, along with gallons of Elmer's glue, and I happened to be hyperfocused at times, so I decided to turn them all into bricks. lol No one ever punished me for that. I'm not sure anyone understood what had happened. lol

I also stole myself a rock collection courtesy of the closet. lol I think a few stones were from one of those science sets that came with a magnifying glass. I think I remember a couple having numbered stickers on them. I let kids play with them, so I no longer have the ones with the stickers to check, but I do have a few other stones left.

I remember feeling like a piece of shit. The teacher's husband was some sort of law enforcement officer, and he would act like an authority figure; he'd come in to visit, then give me shit about not being able to sit still or lecture me about asking too many questions.

I've had this Labyrinth in the picture since 5th grade. I loved the movie, and I loved this book. I used to bring it into the closet all the time. I even wrote a book report on it
 On so many occasions, I had wished that a portal would open up in the wall and allow me to enter another world, so I wouldn't be trapped in that stupid fucking closet anymore. lol



December
This Christmas was awesome. I got the GI Joe Nightforce Hovercraft Night Striker and Mobile Command Center. I felt like I was going to save the world. lol I got all the Night Force figures, and the Night Shade sub. I spent countless hours with those in the basement and backyard.
