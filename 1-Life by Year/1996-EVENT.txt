 



1996

When we came back for the from Christmas vacation, I was still bored out of my mind, we were always trying to come up with something to entertain ourselves, there was never anything to do, but sit around and find that something ourselves. I don't 40 plus days of being suspended taught me anything, expect to ignore my problems. lol

I'm not sure whose idea it was originally, it was probably mine, lol Either way, a few of use decided to take walkie talkies from the utility closet and see if we could cause some harmless trouble.

We thought our teachers and the police officers that the school hired as security were a bunch of buffoons.

 One day early before schools started, a few of us picked the lock to the shop, while the others watched for teachers, then picked our way into utility closet, took a couple walkie talkies and started having fun

I had way to much fun doing this, at the time a vice principal had recently fallen and broken their arm, this was after chasing kids who were skipping class. lol Seems like giving kids detention is worth running down stairs in dress shoes. lol

So I took this opportunity to moan into the walkie talkie, I thought that pretending I was hurt was hilarious. 

One time one someone asked what I needed, I replied " I need a man to hug and kiss'. lol 

We' tell the people trying to help us that "we're trapped' " Please help, they've got us" lol

Eventually somebody ratted us out and even though I didn't get caught with a walkie talkie on me, I got suspended for 10 days, because I had been accused, not because I actually got caught. lol

This point in my life I was a anxiety ridden disaster, a hilarious disaster, but still a disaster. 
we were a bunch of punks. lol  I was still quite rambunctious and imaginative.







01-17-1996






After the Walkie Talkie incident, and me getting amazing grade after the 30 day suspension, I think the school had enough of all the fun and excitement, plus they weren't happy that my grade were great at this point. I was able to improve all of my so called "failing grades" and was getting A's on everything, so school officials offered me the opportunity to go to night school. They said all I needed to do was to take one English class and I could graduate with the rest of my class. I was so excited, I accepted.

I remember thinking the class was dumb and I didn't take it seriously. I obviously passed, but my final paper was on why I thought English class was stupid. lol 
I remember the teacher that my paper was written really well and trying to be supportive, like he was trying to talk me into enjoying to write.
Clearly not one of my brightest moments, I'm not sure what I was thinking, seeing I ended up needing to take a review for college writing when I sign up for STCC in the future, lol I guess I wasn't as smart as I thought. lol

I can't remember exactly why I was in the principal's office for this incident, but needless to say I didn't respect the police at this time, my friend Tammy had recently died and there were still no answers, cops were acting weird around the school. That one weirdo had popped out of sub basement try to act like I was attempting to go down there. lol 



01-25

Looking back, I'm surpised that no one took this more seriously. Granted these situations were all just jokes to me, but Tammy had died a little over a year before and no one knew what happened.

November

I went with my brother, and I'm not sure who else, to the movie theater in Enfield and we saw Star Trek First Contact. I remember being so excited for some massive space and then it lasted all of 30 seconds, what a let down, but what we got was still cool. lol Deep Space Nine was still on at this time, so I was always hoping for a cross over.
