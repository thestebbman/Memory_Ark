SUMMARY
Born January 22, 1959, in Chicopee. Died June 13, 2022, at his home in
West Warren, with his family by his side (obituary, Legacy.com). He coached
Ricky's youth soccer team, worked at Hoppe Technologies in Chicopee in the
1980s and later in technical support and field service for Mazak Corp. He
played mandolin and guitar, fished, metal-detected, collected coins, and -
in the obituary's words - never met a cookie he didn't like.

WHAT THE RECORD HOLDS
- 1986: Coached Ricky's youth soccer team with his brother, Uncle Mark.
  Photo caption in the 1986 year file: "My dad Rick on the right."
  (Ricky's year files)
- 1987: Worked at Hoppe Technologies, a machine shop on 1st Avenue in
  Chicopee. Ricky loved going along for the after-work pickup rides.
  (Ricky's year files)
- 1987: Driving to pick his father up from Hoppe Technologies, Ricky saw
  a car hit a street-lamp pole at Robbin Road and Page Boulevard and
  described it vividly. His father asked why he could describe that event
  so well but not his school reports. Ricky says that question stuck with
  him into 2026. (Ricky's 1987 file; his revised 1987-EVENT-2)
- Later work: technical support and field service for Mazak Corp.
  (Obituary, Legacy.com - a later job than Hoppe; both are in the record,
  dated as far as the record dates them.)
- January 22, 1959 - June 13, 2022: born in Chicopee to David and
  Elizabeth (Picard) Stebbins; died at home in West Warren, age 63, with
  his family by his side. Ricky's own record (2026-09-22) says his father
  died of cancer in 2022; the obituary does not state a cause in the text
  retrieved. Both stand as written; neither is edited to match the other.
  (Obituary, Legacy.com; Ricky's words, 2026-09-22)
- Family, from the obituary: his wife Susan; his children Ricky Stebbins,
  Bobby Stebbins (and his wife Erica), and Kathy Stebbins, and their
  mother Linda Stebbins; stepchildren Candyce Weagle, Christopher Weagle,
  James Weagle, and Adam Cooper; siblings Mike Stebbins, Mary Westcott,
  and Mark Stebbins; predeceased by his brother David; eight grandchildren:
  Dominic, Lily, Lucas, Lacey, Charlotte, Madeline, Clarice, and Colton.
  Visitation and funeral June 25, 2022, Sampson's Chapel of the Acres,
  21 Tinkham Road, Springfield. (Obituary, Legacy.com)
