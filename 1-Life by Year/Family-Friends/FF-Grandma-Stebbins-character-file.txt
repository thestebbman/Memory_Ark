SUMMARY
Until today, the record held her without a name: "Grandma Stebbins," the
Moss Road grandmother, at whose house the family gathered. Her son's
obituary (2022) names her - Elizabeth (Picard) Stebbins. Ricky's own
record about her is plain and stays plain in this file: he was never
close with her, and he says so. A character file that only flatters is
not his record.

WHAT THE RECORD HOLDS
- Family picnics and gatherings at Grandma and Grandpa Stebbins' house
  on Moss Road, including after Ricky's First Communion in 1986 and
  Thanksgiving visits. Ricky's 1986 words: going over there was a blast -
  freeze tag, playing GI Joe on the stone wall in the backyard with a
  little fire pit built into it. (Ricky's 1986 file)
- After Grandpa Stebbins died, Grandma Stebbins and Aunt Mary took Ricky
  and cousins Jeremy and Andrea to the Bronx Zoo, while other cousins
  went elsewhere (his brother and Joey to the Statue of Liberty).
  Ricky's own words in the 1988 file: "I was never close with my grandma
  Stebbins or my Aunt Mary. I don't recall being excited about this trip
  or looking forward to another. This was also the only trip with Grandma
  Stebbins or Aunt Mary." (Ricky's 1988 file)
- A Stebbins-side family memoir draft exists in the archive
  (FF-Father's_Side_of_the_Family-Draft.txt), centered on Grandma and
  Grandpa Stebbins, Moss Road, family picnics, and cousins. That draft is
  separate from this file and is not changed by it.
- Named in her son's obituary (2022) as "the late David and Elizabeth
  (Picard) Stebbins." She was already gone by June 2022. (Obituary of
  Richard L. Stebbins Sr., Legacy.com)
