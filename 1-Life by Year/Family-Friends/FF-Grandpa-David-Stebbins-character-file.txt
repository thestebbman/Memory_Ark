CHARACTER FILE - DAVID STEBBINS ("GRANDPA")

Who this is: Ricky's grandfather. The janitor at Our Lady of the Sacred
Heart, Ricky's Catholic school - and, in Ricky's telling, the adult who
made him feel safe there. Father of Richard L. Stebbins Sr. (see Dad's
file). Husband of Elizabeth (Picard) Stebbins (see Grandma Stebbins'
file).

Filed: November 1, 2026
Last updated: November 1, 2026

SUMMARY
The school janitor who was also a shield. When priests were rough with
Ricky - slamming him against walls, dragging him down stairs, belittling
him for questioning what they taught - his grandfather appearing in the
hallways made them treat Ricky better and kept him from being hurt worse.
Ricky got in trouble on purpose to be sent to him. He died of lung cancer
while Ricky was in elementary school, and in Ricky's words, "No one ever
wanted to talk about it."

WHAT THE RECORD HOLDS
- Worked as janitor at Our Lady of the Sacred Heart. His presence in the
  hallways interrupted priests mistreating Ricky and kept him safer.
  (Ricky's year files; the OLSH file, 1984-1991)
- Ricky would get in trouble deliberately so he could visit him and help
  set up for Bingo: walking the gym, carrying chairs, moving tables. It
  felt important, and it was a break from the teachers and priests.
  (Ricky's year files)
- 1987: A family hike up the Mount Tom ski slope, with his grandfather
  and uncles, is where Ricky found his love of hiking. (Ricky's files)
- His death, in Ricky's own words from the revised 1988 file: after
  Grandpa Stebbins died, "I never stayed to set up for Bingo again after
  he passed away," and "once my grandfather had passed away, I didn't
  have much protection from the adult nonsense." (1988 file, revised)
- His son's obituary (2022) names him: "the late David and Elizabeth
  (Picard) Stebbins," of Chicopee. (Obituary of Richard L. Stebbins Sr.,
  Legacy.com)
