SUMMARY
The record holds the least about her, and what it holds matters most to
Ricky. In his words (2026-10-29), she "got the shit end of the stick and
suffered in a nursing home for years." The rest of her file is mostly
open space - held for the story Ricky intends to tell about her one day,
at his timing.

WHAT THE RECORD HOLDS
- Thanksgiving visits in Ricky's 1986 file mention going over to "my
  grandma Francis / aunt Flossie's" as one of the holiday stops,
  alongside Grandpa and Grandma Stebbins on Moss Road. (Ricky's 1986
  file)
- Her only other appearance in the record is an open question in his
  1989 file: "I think Grandma Francis went to visit them there, but
  I'll have to find out" - the "them there" being Cape Cod, with a
  Kansas thread attached that he has not verified. That question stays
  open here. Nothing is claimed about Kansas or Cape Cod beyond it.
  (Ricky's 1989 file)
- 2026-10-29, in Ricky's own words and framing: she suffered in a
  nursing home for years, and one day he wants to go into detail about
  how he FEELS that could and should have been prevented. He explicitly
  does NOT blame the rest of the family for not stepping up back then:
  he was suffering invisible issues himself, and "you never know what
  people were going through or what burdens they're carrying." His
  stance: sometimes we just have to make the best of these bad
  situations and hope to learn something from them to prevent future
  issues. (Ricky, 2026-10-29, recorded the same day)
