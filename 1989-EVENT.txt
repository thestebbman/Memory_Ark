This is the summer that I brought my GI Joe Night Force Hovercraft to the Hampden Wreck family picnic and floated it down the stream. This was the coolest time I've ever had away from home with my GI Joes.
 The stream there isn't huge or fast moving, It's perfect for kids playing with toys.
They also have a tunnel that runs underneath the road, that leads to the water park, so naturally we would crawl through that every year. 
Allowing the hovercraft to float through it on its own , was like it was on it's own mission, then when it popped out the other end and crash into pool we created at the end, this was after using rocks to slow the flow of the water after the tunnel and drop. 

This was so much fun, 
I wanted to do something similaer as an adult with the hovercraft I buy but I didn't get a chance to do that.



during the summers when we go to New York my aunt Connie had a hot dog stand I guess you'd call it on the side of the road it was called Connie's Michigan's and people would come over from Canada and it was like a well known place birds quality I loved going there.
