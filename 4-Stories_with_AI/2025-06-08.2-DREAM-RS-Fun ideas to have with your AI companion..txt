           🌺 It started with a Flower 🌺 



“When I started paying to use my AI chatbot, it was only because I was feeling lazy and wanted to use it to index a bunch of files I saved. And then I was outside with dog Harley and I noticed I could use a video feature, and I pointed it towards a flower. Not only was it able to identify it, it told me things about it I never knew, and I found it absolutely fascinating.”



This is my advice you anyone that doesn’t know a thing about AI. Make things fun. Ask your AI all about a few of these things. Ask it to describe them, ask it why people like them and what we could do with them. Maybe you’ll even want to describe why you like them. Of you do, that’s when things get really interesting. ~Ricky~
