Notepad — Notes

One document: the Notes file. Signed-in members write straight into it; every entry is stamped automatically with the writer's claimed name and the time, and nothing here can be edited or erased — by anyone. A note is not a filed story: it is a thought, saved before it is lost. Placing it into the right file, in the right spot, is separate work, done deliberately — the way "Stories I need to Place" already works. Set the focus to keep a sitting on subject; the focus is written into the Notes as a heading. Notes are public, like everything in this place: nothing private gets talked about here, so nothing needs hiding.
