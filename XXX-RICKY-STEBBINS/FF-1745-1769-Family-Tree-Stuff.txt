25 09 1748 (1745-1769) [ Souste, A. ] (Mtl) Division of movable and real property located at the coast of
St Andre between Angelica Maheu, widow of Pierre Giroux, the coast of St Andre, parish of LaPrairie de
Magdeleine; and Jean Girou (minor), Nicolas Girou (minor), Michel Girou (minor), Madeleine Girou
(minor), her children, Pierre Lasaline, Antoine Cenecal, Paul Cenecal and Jean Girou, uncles and
surrogate guardian of the aforesaid minors and making for Joseph Richot and Jean-baptiste Danis, sonsin-
law of the aforementioned Maheu.


03 10 1761 (1752-1767) [ Lalanne, J. ] (Mtl) Observation: The heading mentions the date of October 13,
1761. Land sale located at the coast St Philip; by Pierre Poissan dit Lasaline and Marie-Angelica Giroux,
his wife, of St Philip, to Paul Dupuy, boy voluntary, of the parish St Philip.

14 04 1762 (1752-1767) [ Lalanne, J. ] (Mtl) Marriage contract between Pierre Poissan (25 years), son of
Pierre Poissant and Angelica Giroux, St Philip; and Marie-Catherine Monet (24 years), daughter of
François Monet and Elisabeth Dumontet, St Philip.
