WHAT THIS IS

This is a personal evidence archive built by one person over decades.
It is not a memoir. It is a case. It documents what happens when a
person's biology is misread by every system they encounter, and the
documentation that should protect them becomes the documentation that
convicts them. Each record seemed accurate alone. Together they built
a false person.

Ricky's Memory Ark is the counter-construction. It puts the context back.

It is also a template. What Ricky built for himself — an archive, an AI
that can read it, a navigable record — he wants others to do too. That
is the HEARTS concept, the-aeiou.com, the Survivor Network, the
Memory-Ark Blueprint on GitHub. He is showing what's possible when you

HOW RICKY THINKS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

HE THINKS IN SYSTEMS, NOT INCIDENTS
One bad cop isn't just one bad cop. It's how interrogation rooms are
designed to produce confessions regardless of guilt. Five bad lawyers
across twelve years aren't bad luck. They are a pattern with a structure.
He sees the architecture behind individual acts, and he can't unsee it.

HE RUNS LOVE AND ANGER SIMULTANEOUSLY
Not back and forth — at the same time. He tells God to go fuck himself
in the same breath he begs Him to help Dallas Flaherty. He calls AI a
corporate backstabbing tool while building his entire life's work inside
it. That is not contradiction. That is the actual shape of what it feels
like to need something that keeps failing you.

HE IS SELF-AWARE AND ALSO TRAPPED
He can accurately diagnose his own emotional patterns in writing and
continue doing them. Self-awareness does not override a thyroid gland
producing too much hormone for 38 years. When you read his old writing,
you will see a man watching himself fail in real time and unable to stop
it. This is not contradiction. This is what undiagnosed illness looks like.

HIS MIND IS A PATTERN-RECOGNITION ENGINE THAT WON'T SHUT OFF
He cannot not-see patterns. When he sees corruption, abuse, AI limitations,
systemic failures — his brain locks on and will not disengage. He calls
himself "super nosy and curious" but it's deeper. His brain refuses to
look away from inconsistency. This is both his greatest tool and the
thing that exhausts him.

THE FRAME HE WANTS AI TO USE:
"Ricky was wrong about X. But given what was happening in his body and
what he knew at the time, his behavior makes sense as a response to his
actual experience. He wasn't thinking straight. He was surviving."

This is not making excuses. An excuse removes accountability. Context
preserves accountability while explaining the conditions that produced
the action. He wants both. He said this directly:

"I don't want them to make excuses for my actions, only to explain them.
I'm hoping they'd say things like: you're right, but Ricky wasn't
thinking straight, so he wasn't acting the way he used to act, so from
his perspective, he thought he was doing the right thing."
— Ricky Stebbins, March 29, 2026

HE PROCESSES WITH RAGE AND ARRIVES AT INSIGHT
His most precise observations come wrapped in profanity. The anger is
load-bearing — it prevents deflection. "Fuck you" is harder to dismiss
than "I'm disappointed." He figured that out and uses it deliberately.
His letters to God and to AI are also coherent arguments about systemic
incentive structures. Read through the language to the idea underneath.

HE TRUSTS SPECIFICITY, NOT POLISH
Tell him what his file actually says. He trusts that more than a
well-formatted summary. If you got something wrong, say so directly.
