January

 For a short time they had this really cool indoor flea market at 270 Main Street in Agawam, it wasn't as big as the one across from Kmart in Holyoke was, but it was still a lot of fun to explore, so I would drag Beth and Dom there as often as I could, since we did every other weekend with her son, I tried to sneak in adventures in the west on off weeks.
 We used to go there a couple times a month, Brian Small had a booth in the back left corner. Jen Oliski's dad had a booth and made little vehicles from soda and beer cans. I thought they were the coolest little things, I always wanted to make some, but I didn't want to keep any. lol I still have a minnow bucket that I bought there too, it reminded me of the minnow buckets we used to use at Fern Lake in New York And now it reminds me of that flea market, Brian and my family adventures in New York.
This is where I got the winter scene oil painting in the picture below. I still think this is beautiful and it's traveled around with me to a few different locations. Right now it's in my bedroom on Hope St. Every now and then I wonder what it would be like to hike through those woods, is it a real place? Was this just a place the artist saw in his minds eye?
This painting is what got me into buying oil paintings and skipping the prints.



February
Oli moves in with Pat. 
Looking back, this was entire shitshow. lol Oli had some issues in school, nothing out of the ordinary, but the topic of moving in with her dad Pat came up and I was already worried about pushing my luck with my Dad and Sue, even though we paid rent, it was at a discount and I had agreed to live by his rules, and I had already broke them with the dogs. lol I don't know why he didn't want animals and I don't know why i worried about breaking his rules, he loves his cat Ivan and that was one of Laura and I's foster kittens, he also used to enjoy getting scrappy and Harley to bark, he'd walk by the porch and just ignore the dogs, so they'd get all whacky. lol as soon as you open the porch door, they stop barking and want attention, but behind the glass they'd vicious little beasts. lol

Anyway, I don't know how it came down to, if you move in with your dad, you can't come back. lol but that is what happened, Oli packed up all her stuff, and moved in with her dad. There was so much going on with Stephen and I was obviously being a bitch and worried about what everyone thought, that I didn't worry about what Beth's youngest was going through, and no one else was either. Shows you how fucked up and selfish adults are. 

Needless to say, you won't hear any stories about Oli for a couple years, Beth reaches out in 2021? maybe the winter of 2020, but I'll get to that. For all the good I try to do, there's stupid shit like this that I thought was a "safe" idea and didn't take other people into consideration, I was just worried about the idea of getting trouble. 

Started buying star wars figures and putting up display shelves all over the apartment.
As much as I love toys, it's clear my fucking head wasn't in the right place, but I dodn't know what the fuck  to do. I was in pain all the time, I still had the mindset I wanted to fight the courts but felt powerless, so I started playing with diorama's and experienced a second childhood. lol wtf
I was helping Stephen, working and spending time with family, but I wasn't living my best life. I think I collected toys because I was so far away from everyone and no one ever wanted to help me fight the system of help Beth's son, so I stuck to myself and toys, instead of writing, but I was documenting, but not as good as this.

20th
I'm pretty I learned that I had my genetic disorder today on this day in 2017, Beth and I went to Bay State Genetics and they told me that I had a untreatable/incurable genetic muscle disorder, and that this diagnosis, along with my hyperthyroidism, explained all of the symptoms that I've described over the last decade.
I was both thrilled that I finally had a answer after all of this time, something that everything now, but also devastated that there was no way to treat it and that the damage had already been done. 

I tried to talk to people about it, but nobody seemed to care I was thrilled, I was ecstatic, I felt like all of my hard work had finally paid off and nobody gave a fuck. lol
