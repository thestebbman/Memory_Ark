December

Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles 2 the arcade game came out for the Nintendo Entertainment System. 

I remember the weather was terrible, we just got tons of snow a couple days after Christmas. I'm assuming family matters prevented me from venturing out sooner, but I finally had enough money for TMNT 2, so I walked to Kmart, it was only about a mile up the street. I was so excited to play this version for the NES. We played video games all the time, but I really loved the ninja turtles and this was supposed to be exactly like the one I sprained my wrist playing at the mall a few years earlier. I don't think anyone else got as excited as I did. I remember being worried the cartridge might get wet, since it was still snowing on my walk. lol  

A day or so later my mom drove me to Pizza Hut on Boston Rd (where Tacobell is now). The game came with a coupon for a free personal pizza and I was so excited, Pizza Hut was a treat as a kid, so getting a free pizza with a game was like winning the lottery. Like a dork, on the way out I told them I wasn't coming back for the rest of the year.
