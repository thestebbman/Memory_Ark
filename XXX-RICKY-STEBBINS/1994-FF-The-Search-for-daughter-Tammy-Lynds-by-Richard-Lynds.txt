having a conversation with her sister about what was going on that night. Here follows the story of events that starts with her talk with her sister to the time she was finally

found November 4, 1994, along Fox Road.

Tammy could not unlock the gate located on the North side of the yard because she had the wrong gate key. She came back in the front door, went back up stairs to her room, where Allison had quickly ran back to and got into bed, she looked for the correct gate key and left again out the front door. Allison stayed at the window until Tammy was out of sight down Lamont Street. The two guys did not follow Tammy down Lamont Street during the time that Allison was at

the window. Allison then went to bed.

July 25, 1994

After Allison came back home I asked her again where Tammy said she was going. This time I took Allison with me to Grayson Drive in our van and parked it at the end of Duffy Lane. We both went along the top of the bank along Grayson Drive from the south towards the north. We got to where the bank ends and went down the bank towards the

put us out around the bend on Grayson Drive south of the van. (What I didn't know was that we were just 300 feet away from

where Tammy was found along Fox Road.) We then both headed home.

July 26,1994

time :

3:24

only file this as a run away not as a missing person.

July 26 -July 29, 1994 On one of these days I went over to Ricky Stebbins house to ask him about Tammy going over to his house in the late evening of July 14 to the early morning of July 15, 1994. also about her meeting him in the woods in the late evening of July 21 to the early morning of July 22, 1994. Ricky said that neither of these things happened and that he had not seen her for a couple of days. He said this while his mother was standing in their front door. Ricky stood out on the front steps while we were talking about Tammy being missing. He said he knew nothing

about it. I then went back home.

Feb. 7, 1995

Sue and I were asked to come down to the police station at 10:00 .m. to talk to the police about their case about Tammy. We rought along all of the paperwork of information that we both had accumulated within the past six months. We were planning to hand all of this over to the police to help with the

investigation of who killed our daughter Tammy. When we got there they asked Sue to go in first to talk to the police. Then after Sue they asked me to come in to talk to the police. What I didn't know until I got into the back room that we were both being interrogated by the police as suspects in the murder of our daughter Tammy. The police never asked if we had any information that could help them, just that they knew that I was their prime suspect in the death of Tammy by reports that were given to them. Before they were done with me I gave them a copy of this letter, which at that time ended with the entry on Jan. 12, 1995 and my conclusion. They asked me if I was willing to take a lie detector test the following morning at the Springfield registry, I said "No problem, I'1l

be there." When Sue and I met in the hallway of the station we couldn't believe that they felt I killed Tammy and Sue was covering up

for me. We left confused and troubled.

Feb. 7, 1995 After getting home Sue called our friend down our street for advice. Both her and her husband came over and we explained what happened that morning. I contacted the lawyer that they told us about and made a appointment with him the following morning

before I was to meet the police at the registry.

Feb. 8, 1995 At the lawyers office we both explained what happened from the

day Tammy was missing to the day before at the police station. He called the police station and told them that I was not going to meet the police at the registry that morning and if they had any charges against me that I will come down to the police

Since that day we have talked to three other lawyers and no lawyer is willing to help us with our problem. One went as far as stating that he would not get involved in this case because it was to hot of a case. Everyone that we talked to has advised us not to do anything to upset the police, not to make any statements publicly, or try to do anything special like a memorial in regards

to the death of our daughter Tammy. I'm sorry, but Tammy was our daughter and we will not just sit back and wonder for the rest of our lives what happened to her, we loved her very much. We want to try all avenues possible to

help us solve how Tammy died and who killed her.

The end result I feel is that all along Susan and I have been correct in our feelings about this and the police have been wrong all along from day one!!!!!!!!!!
