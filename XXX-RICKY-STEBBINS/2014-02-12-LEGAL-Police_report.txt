Call #14-19633:
On Wednesday, 12 February 2014, at about 0930 hours, Richard Stebbins walked into the Hampden County Courthouse suffering from stab wounds to the right side of his chest and to his right wrist. He reported that while en route to take a bus to court this date, (near the corner of Hope and Phillips,) to extend a restraining order against a Kishana Johnson, he was set upon by two burly black males who stopped near him in a black SUV, and stabbed him in his chest and arm and struck him in the right side of his face near the eyebrow, before
fleeing along Boston Road. Stebbins, despite his wounds, caught a bus and went to court as he had intended.
Judge William Boyle, who was scheduled to hear his case, has been notified of this attack. AMR #417 (D. Pepin and A. Gaskins,) responded to the courthouse, treated the roughly three inch laceration to the right side of Stebbins' chest and to his right wrist, and transported him to the Baystate
Emergency Department where he was taken under the care of Dr. Dorothy Jacques, MD.
