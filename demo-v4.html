The Workstation Demo — try it yourself

This is what the old apps could do, and what we are asking be restored: a person walks their own files as a tree and chooses what the AI holds, while a meter shows the cost of every choice. That control is the protection — an AI handed everything at once drowns; an AI handed the right files, by the person who knows them, can actually help. Nothing here is a picture of the idea — it works. Try it.

The character files below are real (shortened for the demo) — they were filed in the public archive this morning. Practice notes live only in this page, only while it is open — never saved, never sent anywhere.

The notepad — one Notes file

Write a note (a memory, a correction, anything) and file it. It goes to exactly one place — the Notes file: dated, in order, and nothing in it can be edited or erased. A note is not a placement. If it belongs in someone's character file, moving it there is a separate, deliberate step, taken by the file's owner. One destination is what keeps notes from being scattered everywhere — and what keeps the record trustworthy.
